## Important actionable finding

Q11, `2020-21-shk-mosobl-52`, Russian glosses o2/o4 need context-preserving wording:

- o2 `Можно пользоваться общими палочками и ложками` leaves out the critical serving-utensil meaning and can be understood as sharing eating utensils. Propose `Можно пользоваться общими палочками и ложками для перекладывания еды — ими не едят и не касаются рта`. This also adds useful knowledge beyond a bare translation, as CLAUDE.md requests. The already attached Jiangmen source explicitly says 分餐工具 … 不直接接触就餐者嘴部. Independent corroboration: https://snamr.shaanxi.gov.cn/sy/gzdt/sxdt/202003/t20200318_2598167.html .
- o4 `Можно выбирать кусочки еды в тарелке` makes ordinary choosing sound forbidden and weakens the intended negative assertion. Propose `Можно перебирать еду в тарелке, выбирая кусочки`. Keep Chinese `可以在盘子里挑菜` exactly unchanged. Official etiquette guidance distinguishes taking food from picking through/stirring it: https://www.ningguo.gov.cn/News/show/1199279.html and https://www.fdwmw.gov.cn/portal/article/index.html?id=23764 .
- False-assertion glosses legitimately remain translations, not endorsed etiquette rules. Their `url:null` is intentional and is not a finding.
